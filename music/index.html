About

I’m a trombonist focused on live, ensemble playing. I’ve played in a youth jazz orchestra, a brass band and a university big band, and now play ska with The Hoplites in Nottingham.

Bands

The Hoplites

Current · Nottingham · Ska, rocksteady & reggae

I play trombone in the horn section of The Hoplites, a nine-piece band drawing on first-wave ska, rocksteady and reggae. The band debuted with a double-headline show at Metronome in September 2024, followed by the River of Change maxi single and three singles in 2025.

The band has supported The Beat, Sonic Boom Six, Catbite and King Prawn, and played venues including Rock City, Metronome, The Bodega and New Cross Inn.
